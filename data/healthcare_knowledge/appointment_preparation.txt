GENERAL APPOINTMENT PREPARATION

Before a healthcare appointment, patients may want to:

1. Review the reason for the appointment and any instructions
   provided by the healthcare organization.

2. Bring or have access to a current medication and supplement list,
   including names and doses when known.

3. Bring relevant medical records, referral information, test results,
   or previous healthcare documents when requested.

4. Prepare a list of questions or concerns to discuss with the
   healthcare professional.

5. Bring identification, insurance information, and appointment
   details when required by the healthcare organization.

6. Follow any specific preparation instructions provided by the
   healthcare professional or healthcare facility.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE

General preparation guidance does not replace instructions from a
healthcare professional or healthcare facility.

If preparation instructions conflict with general information,
the patient's healthcare provider or healthcare facility should
be contacted for clarification.

This information is for general educational purposes and does not
provide diagnosis, treatment, or medication recommendations.
